🛒 E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an E-commerce sales dataset using Python to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, payment preferences, and other important business insights.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), KPI analysis, and data visualization to transform raw transaction data into meaningful insights that can support business decision-making.

---

🎯 Objectives

- Clean and prepare the raw E-commerce dataset.
- Analyze overall sales and transaction performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze payment method preferences.
- Study customer age distribution and its relationship with sales.
- Identify unusual or invalid values in the dataset.
- Generate meaningful business insights and recommendations.

---

🛠️ Technologies & Libraries Used

- Python
- Pandas – Data cleaning and analysis
- NumPy – Numerical operations
- Matplotlib – Data visualization
- Seaborn – Statistical visualization
- Jupyter Notebook – Development environment

---

📊 Key KPIs Analyzed

The project analyzes several important business KPIs, including:

- Total Sales Revenue
- Total Number of Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Average Customer Age
- Average Quantity per Order
- Category-wise Sales
- Product-wise Revenue
- Average Product Rating
- Payment Method Distribution
- Customer Age vs Sales
- Duplicate Transactions
- Invalid Quantities and Ratings

💰 Sales Calculation

Sales revenue was calculated using:

Sales = Quantity × Unit_Price

---

🧹 Data Cleaning

The dataset was prepared before performing analysis.

Key data-cleaning activities included:

- Checking for missing values.
- Identifying duplicate transactions.
- Validating data types.
- Checking order dates for correctness.
- Identifying invalid quantities.
- Checking product ratings for invalid values.
- Detecting potential outliers.
- Creating calculated sales/revenue columns.
- Preparing categorical and numerical variables for analysis.

---

📈 Exploratory Data Analysis

Several visualizations were created to understand the dataset.

1. Category-wise Sales

A bar chart was used to compare sales revenue across different product categories.

2. Category-wise Average Rating

Analyzed the average customer rating for each product category.

3. Top Products vs Revenue

Identified products contributing significantly to overall revenue.

4. Payment Mode Analysis

A pie chart was used to understand customer preferences across different payment methods.

5. Customer Age Distribution

A histogram was used to understand the distribution of customer ages.

6. Age vs Sales

A scatter plot was used to analyze the relationship between customer age and sales.

---

🔍 Key Insights

The analysis helped identify:

- Which product categories generate the highest revenue.
- Which products are major contributors to sales.
- Customer preferences regarding payment methods.
- Distribution of customers across different age groups.
- Whether customer age has any noticeable relationship with sales.
- Categories receiving higher or lower customer ratings.
- Data-quality issues such as duplicates, invalid values, and outliers.

---

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing categories.
- Promote products with strong sales performance.
- Improve or review low-rated products.
- Optimize payment options based on customer preferences.
- Use customer demographics for targeted marketing campaigns.
- Monitor unusual transactions and data-quality issues.
- Use sales trends to improve inventory and product planning.
